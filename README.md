### About

I turn the processes an institution runs on into governed digital systems, and I build them myself. At the English Language Institute (ELI), Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University, I am the sole architect and developer of the core systems for performance evaluation, scheduling, quality observation, curriculum tracking and task tracking. I design the process first, build the system, govern it from day one, and train the people who use it.
